**Key result.** On the original code the agent's cap was not a loss limit. A compromised or buggy agent key, working with any filler, could drain the **whole** desk escrow, not just the cap (H-01, PoC below). After the fix, fills are priced at no less than oracle −1% (owner-tunable, max 10%), and the cap counts filled volume over a rolling 24h window. A malicious agent can now lose at most `fillDiscountBps × cap` per token per day. On Base, prices are refused while the L2 sequencer is down or has just restarted (M-01).

## 4. Findings

### H-01: Unbounded signed limit plus open-only cap lets agent and filler drain the full escrow (Fixed)
- **Location:** `LimitDesk.fill` / `_fillPrice` (limit and stop-limit pricing), `post` (cap check).
- **Issue:** A limit order filled *at the signed limit* whenever `oracle >= limit`, with no bound on how far below the oracle the limit could be. The per-agent cap only limited *currently open* sell size: a fill unlocked it, so the agent could post again right away.
- **Impact:** A compromised or buggy agent signs `limitPriceX18 = 1` and an accomplice fills, over and over. PoC on the original code: with a 1 WETH cap the desk lost **10/10 WETH for < 100 wei USDC** in 10 post+fill loops. The owner loses everything escrowed, which defeats the main safety promise ("within owner caps"). Confidence 100.
- **Fix:** (1) Fill price = `max(limit, oracle × (1 − fillDiscountBps))` for limit and stop-limit orders. `fillDiscountBps` defaults to 100 (1%), the owner sets 0–1000 with `setFillDiscountBps`, and market stops still fill at the oracle. (2) The cap now covers open + filled sell volume per (agent, epoch, token) over a rolling `CAP_WINDOW = 1 days`. Cancels free the cap; fills do not until the window rolls. New view: `filledInWindowOf`.
- **Regression tests:** `test_H01_dustLimitCannotDrainEscrow`, `test_H01_capWindowResetsAfterOneDay`, `test_H01_cancelFreesCapButFillDoesNot`, `test_H01_setFillDiscountBps`, `testFuzz_H01_fillNeverBelowDiscountedOracle`. Three existing tests were updated to the new fill price (2900 → 2970 and 2400 → 2425.5).
- **Behaviour change for Lef:** fillers now get at most a 1% discount to Chainlink instead of the whole gap to the limit.

### M-01: No Chainlink L2 sequencer uptime check on Base (Fixed)
- **Location:** `oraclePriceX18` / `_usd`.
- **Impact:** During a Base sequencer outage, transactions can still be forced in through L1 while Chainlink can't update. A price up to 2h old is accepted (`ETH_HEARTBEAT`), so a filler can buy escrow at a stale price after a sharp move. This is rated Medium in the audit-kb matches.
- **Fix:** `sequencerFeed` (immutable) is set to Base's feed `0xBCF85224fc0756B9Fa45aA7892530B47e10b6433` when `block.chainid == 8453`, and to address(0) elsewhere, so the constructor and factory signatures are unchanged. Prices revert with `SequencerDown` or `SequencerGracePeriod` (1h after a restart). I checked the feed live on Base (description "L2 Sequencer Uptime Status Feed", answer 0).
- **Regression tests:** `test_M01_noSequencerFeedOffBase`, `test_M01_fillRevertsWhileSequencerDown`, `test_M01_fillRevertsInGracePeriodThenWorks`. The Base fork tests now run against the real sequencer feed.

### L-01: Residual bounded extraction (Acknowledged)
A malicious agent with a colluding filler can still take up to `fillDiscountBps` of `cap` per token per 24h (for example 1% of 5 WETH a day). Keep caps small, set the discount to the lowest value fillers accept, and revoke on anomalies.

### L-02: Fixed heartbeats are loose for Base (Acknowledged)
`ETH_HEARTBEAT = 2h` and `STABLE_HEARTBEAT = 25h` fit Ethereum. Base ETH/USD updates every 20 min or on a 0.15% move, so 2h tolerates a stopped feed for longer than it needs to. M-01 covers the sequencer case. Consider chain-specific heartbeats in a later version.

### L-03: Market stops have no filler margin, and fills can be timed to oracle lag (Acknowledged)
A pure stop fills at the exact oracle price, so it may sit unfilled unless someone fills it at a loss. Fillers can also pick moments when Chainlink lags the market, within the deviation threshold. Bounded and by design.

### I-01: Slither
Timestamp comparisons, ignored `latestRoundData` fields and cyclomatic complexity are all intended. No other detector fired.

### I-02: Refuted vectors
EIP-712 replay is blocked by the domain (chainId and verifyingContract) plus per-agent nonces, the epoch on revoke, and orderId uniqueness. ERC-1271 goes through OZ `SignatureChecker`. Reentrancy is blocked by `nonReentrant` on every state path, with state updated before transfers and only WETH and USDC accepted. The `_revoke` loop is bounded at 32. `totalLocked` and escrow conservation are covered by the existing invariant test. The decimals math checks out in both directions.

### I-03: Dapp
`web/src/abi.ts` was regenerated. The dapp UI does not yet expose `setFillDiscountBps` or `filledInWindowOf`. That is optional, since the defaults are safe.
